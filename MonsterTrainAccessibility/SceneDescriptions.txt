# Monster Train Accessibility: scene descriptions
#
# Short audio descriptions spoken once at moments a sighted player only watches:
# the game launching, a run starting, arriving at a ring of Hell, a boss entrance,
# a boss turning Relentless, a boss falling, an outer-train boss acting, and the
# end of a run. They are never spoken during automated combat.
#
# Format: one entry per line as   key: text
# The text must stay on a single line. Lines starting with # are comments and
# blank lines are ignored. Boss keys use a scenario asset name prefix: an entry
# applies to every battle whose asset name starts with that prefix.
#
# You can edit these descriptions. Place a copy of this file next to the mod DLL
# in BepInEx/plugins and it overrides the built-in one. Delete a line to silence
# that moment.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Game and run
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

launch: Hell has frozen over. Heaven's armies, led by the traitor Seraph, have broken the Covenant and invaded the underworld, and its fires have gone cold. One flame remains, the Pyre, the last spark of Hell, carried aboard a train crewed by monsters, racing down through the frozen rings to take Hell back.
run_start: The train pulls away with the Pyre burning in its top car. Below it, three floors stand ready for your defenders. The rails stretch down into the frozen depths of Hell, and Heaven's forces will board from the bottom and climb toward the flame.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Rings of Hell
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

ring_1: The train enters Limbo, the outermost ring of Hell. Ice grips everything that once burned, and the long descent toward the heart of Hell begins here.
ring_2: The tracks wind along the Cliffs of Fallen Souls, a ring of sheer drops named for the souls lost to them. Cold wind howls past the train as it hugs the edge.
ring_3: The train crosses Bogwurm's Remains, the vast frozen remains of the Bogwurm stretched across the ring. The rails run straight through what is left of it.
ring_4: The train passes into the Waxen Arches, where great arches loom over the tracks, as if shaped from wax and frozen hard. They pass overhead one after another.
ring_5: The rails cross the Frozen Styx Sea, the waters of the dead locked in ice. Nothing stirs beneath the surface as the train races across it.
ring_6: The train plunges into the Wildwood, a tangled forest of Hell gone still and cold. Branches crowd the tracks on every side.
ring_7: The train runs out across the Scorched Plains, wide open land burned by the old fires of Hell, now dead beneath the frost.
ring_8: The train descends into the Crucible, the deep mines where Hellborne and Clipped once dug crystals together under the Covenant. Now its tunnels echo with the enemy.
ring_9: The train reaches the Frozen Heart, the very core of Hell, locked in ice. The end of the line is close, and Heaven's mightiest wait ahead.
ring_10: With one hundred pact shards gathered aboard, the train pushes past the end of the line into the domain of The Last Divinity. Something ancient and endlessly destructive stirs ahead.
