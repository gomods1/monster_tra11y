# Monster Train Accessibility: scene descriptions
#
# Short audio descriptions spoken once at moments a sighted player only watches:
# the game launching, a run starting, arriving at a ring of Hell, a boss entrance,
# a boss turning Relentless, a boss falling, an outer-train boss acting, and the
# end of a run. They are never spoken during automated combat.
#
# Format: one entry per line as   key: text
# The text must stay on a single line. Lines starting with # are comments and
# blank lines are ignored. Boss keys use a scenario asset name prefix: an entry
# applies to every battle whose asset name starts with that prefix.
#
# You can edit these descriptions. Place a copy of this file next to the mod DLL
# in BepInEx/plugins and it overrides the built-in one. Delete a line to silence
# that moment.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Game and run
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

launch: A frozen battlefield under a swirling teal and orange sky, strewn with swords, hammers and horned skulls. Deep in the ice an ember flares, and a blazing crystal breaks free. A red train belching black smoke races along frozen rails, the glowing Pyre crystal held inside. Heaven's soldiers appear, masked and robed in white, and Hell's monsters answer: a thorned green woman with a staff, a red horned demon and a purple ogre with a hammer. Last, a towering angel spreads golden wings.
run_start: The train rolls out, a black iron locomotive trimmed with golden spikes, a great glowing red plow on its front marked with three golden bars. The Pyre burns in its top car, above three floors waiting for your defenders. Heaven's forces will board from the bottom and climb toward the flame.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Rings of Hell
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

ring_1: The train enters Limbo, the outermost ring of Hell, a dusty orange wasteland of cracked, pebbled ground. The track snakes through it in long, lazy curves.
ring_2: The tracks reach the Cliffs of Fallen Souls, tall pillars of dark rock topped with grass and crusted with ice, rising from a deep chasm. The rails split around a central plateau pitted with dark holes.
ring_3: The train crosses Bogwurm's Remains, a black bog of mud and tar pools frozen pale blue. The giant wurm's remains rise from the ground around the rails, its huge spiked maw gaping open, ringed with teeth.
ring_4: The train enters the Waxen Arches, blue waxy ground where melted arches curl overhead, glowing orange at their tips. A stone chapel with a waterwheel stands among them, its roofs dripping blue wax, its windows lit yellow.
ring_5: The rails cross the Frozen Styx Sea, a field of dark blue ice slabs. Violet light glows in the cracks between them, and in one opening, dark shapes swarm beneath the surface.
ring_6: The train plunges into the Wildwood, a green forest floor of thick grass where curling, thorny vines sprout on every side. Purple growths dot the ground, fog drifts low, and small pits of lava glow orange.
ring_7: The train runs out across the Scorched Plains, black volcanic rock split by rivers of glowing lava. The track twists in tight loops across the stone as steam rises from the cracks.
ring_8: The train descends into the Crucible, the mines where Hellborne and Clipped once dug crystals together. Rails run in deep trenches through brown cobblestone and boulders, past wooden scaffolds, an iron chute on stilts and a black metal pit.
ring_9: The train reaches the Frozen Heart, the core of Hell, a basin of black stone. The track ends on a shattered pillar above a crater of icy blue light, ringed by curved black spires cracked with blue glow.
ring_10: With one hundred pact shards aboard, the train pushes past the end of the line into the domain of The Last Divinity. Something ancient and endlessly destructive stirs ahead.
