# Monster Train Accessibility v8.6.1

The Spanish speech of the mod, retranslated from scratch. Game text is not affected (it comes from the game's own Spanish table).

## Natural Spanish instead of word-for-word

- **Spanish word order.** "Artefacto enemigo", not "Enemigo artefacto". Enemies arriving or dying put the event first and the name last: "Llega un enemigo al piso inferior: Discípulo Protector", "Muere un enemigo en el piso central: Talos, la Arquitecta". In floor lists enemies read "Discípulo Protector (enemigo), 0 de ataque".
- **No more gender guessing.** Card, unit and artifact names come from the game with any gender, so lines are built to fit any name: "Se añade Lanza Helada a tu mano", "Lanza Helada, con mejora".
- **The game's own terms.** Mod speech uses the names the game shows: Ecos Cargados, Infundido, Extraer, Asesino, Aperitivo, Azote, La Última Divinidad, Registros personales. It keeps "mazo" and "mejora" where the game's table says "Cubierta" and "Actualizar".
- **Numbers read cleanly.** "2 de 5 de capacidad" instead of "2/5", "10 de 20 de salud", "Salud de la pira: 76 de 80". Singular forms where there was only a plural: "1 brasa", "1 Fragmento del Pacto", "1 hora", "1 opción disponible".
- **Combat lines** read like Spanish: "Discípulo Protector inflige 5 de daño a Guardia Estigia", "La pira recibe 4 de daño, le quedan 76", "Se activa Talismán de Hierro".
- **Help lists (F1)** consistently address you with the imperative: "Intro: elige la carta y añádela al mazo".
- Turn summaries no longer leave "your" in English when a floor has both kills and one of your wounded units.
