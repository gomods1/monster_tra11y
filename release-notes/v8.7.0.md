# Monster Train Accessibility v8.7.0

The Spanish game translation, revised from top to bottom. The English text is untouched: only the Spanish column changes.

## Names

- **About 200 names fixed.** Literal mistranslations are gone: Thoughtbinder is "Encadenamentes" (it was "Carpeta de Ideas", a ring binder), Overgorger is "Supertragón" (was "Sobregiro", a bank overdraft), Shivering Puffer is "Pez Globo Tiritón", Rogue Wave is "Ola Asesina", Initiate is "Iniciado", Lightwings is "Alas de Luz".
- **No more shared names.** Gorge, Gorger and Glutton were all "Glotón" (now Atracón, Tragón, Glotón); Forever Flame and Eternal Flame were both "Llama Eterna" (now Llama Perpetua and Llama Eterna); Reap and Harvest were both "Cosecha" (now Siega and Cosecha); Permafrost and Preserve were both "Conservar".
- **Puns kept alive.** Imp-portant Work = "Trabajo de Mil Diablillos", Imp-pressive = "Diablillante", Imp-in-a-Box = "Diablillo Sorpresa", Onehorn's Tome = "Tomo de Unicuerno", Cuttlebeard = "Barbasepia", Secret of the Ooze = "El Secreto de los Mocos Verdes", Hunk of Junk = "Montón de Latón", Trap Chute = "Rampa Trampa", Pyre-Gro = "Fertipira", One Track Mind = "Mente de Vía Única", and the boss achievements ("Fel-lecida", "Dédalo por Muerto", "Ta Luego, Talos").
- **Characters' genders follow the English**: Heph la Herrera, Pequeña Fade.

## Terms

- Clipped are now **Alicortados**. Floor is always **piso**, a run is a **partida** (Hell Rush stays "Carrera Infernal").
- Keywords: **Ofrenda** (Offering), **Matanza** (Slay), **Arenga** (Rally), **Resolución** (Resolve), **Infinito** (Endless), **Siega** (Reap), **Permafrost**, **Ligado a la Pira** (Pyrebound), **Inspirar** (Inspire, was left in English), **Debilidad a Hechizos**. The mod's own speech uses the same words.
- Menus: Deck is "Mazo" (was "Cubierta"), Upgrade is "Mejorar" (was "Actualizar"), key names read as on a Spanish keyboard (Intro, Esc, Tabulador, AvPág).

## Text

- Card and artifact rules made exact and consistent ("Inflige 10 de daño", "Roba 2 cartas", "Este artefacto desaparece tras 1 batalla").
- Lore quotes, story events, merchant and battle dialogue rewritten in the game's dark tone, each speaker with their own voice (the Hellhorned brutes speak like brutes, the Stygian creatures hiss again), always addressing you with "tú".
