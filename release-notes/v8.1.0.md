# Monster Train Accessibility v8.1.0

## Scene descriptions, now from the real artwork

The scene descriptions added in 8.0.0 were written from the game's text alone. They are now rewritten from the game's actual artwork and the intro video:

- **Game launch**: follows what the intro video shows, from the frozen battlefield and the blazing crystal breaking free of the ice to the train, Heaven's soldiers, Hell's monsters and the golden-winged angel.
- **Bosses**: each boss's entrance describes how it looks (Talos, Daedalus, Fel, Archus, every incarnation of Seraph, the Pyrewing and The Last Divinity). Where a boss has a different Relentless form, its Relentless line describes the change.
- **Rings**: arriving at each ring describes what that part of Hell looks like on the map.
- **Run start and run end**: the train setting out, and the victory, Last Divinity victory and defeat scenes.

The texts are still in `BepInEx/plugins/SceneDescriptions.txt`, so you can edit them, and they can be turned off in the F9 settings ("Scene descriptions").
