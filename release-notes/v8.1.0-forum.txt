Monster Train Accessibility 8.1.0

A small follow-up to 8.0.0. The scene descriptions are now written from the game's real artwork instead of its text alone.

- The description at game launch now follows what the intro video actually shows.
- Each boss's entrance describes what the boss looks like, and bosses with a different Relentless form describe that change too.
- Arriving at each ring of Hell describes what that ring looks like on the map.
- The train setting out at the start of a run, and the victory and defeat scenes, are described from their artwork.

The texts are in BepInEx\plugins\SceneDescriptions.txt if you want to edit them, and you can switch them off in the F9 settings menu.

Feedback welcome, especially if a description doesn't match what's happening.
