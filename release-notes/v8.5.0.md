# Monster Train Accessibility v8.5.0

Focus and announcement cleanup. Less noise, no wrong announcements.

## Focus no longer stops on things you can't use

- **Battle**: the arrows step over the ember counter and the floor capacity tooltip. Ember is in the turn start line and on G. The floor is on K and in the floor review.
- **Main menu, run dialog**: the ring and clan icons next to Resume are skipped. Resume already says them.
- **Battle intro**: the trial reward panel is skipped. The screen announcement and the trial switch both say the rule and the reward.
- At the edge of a screen, an arrow press that would only land on one of these items stays quiet, like the game's own edges.

## Battle

- **No more focus chatter while combat plays.** After End Turn the game moves focus to the HUD by itself, and the mod used to read it ("Energy count: Ember. Spent to play cards...", later "Draw Pile"). Now only your own arrow presses are read.
- **Spells that target a unit** (Torch, for example) no longer also say "Select floor", and no longer say "Playing on bottom floor" when the spell hits a unit.
- **"Playing on" is gone.** It was spoken even when the game refused the play ("Units not playable in Pyre Chamber"). The summon and damage lines already confirm a real play.
- **Shorter targeting lines**: "Select floor. Bottom floor..." and "Select target. 2 of 3: Disciple Protector...". The keys are in F1 help.
- **Up from your hand to the floors** without a card in hand now says "Floors, Bottom floor..." with what is on that floor. Before, it wrongly started floor targeting ("Select floor... Escape to cancel").
- **The false "Select Card to Upgrade"** at the start of every battle is gone.
- **Pile and End Turn buttons** say "Draw Pile, 13 cards", "Discard Pile, 1 card" and "End Turn". Their tooltip text is in the review buffer (Ctrl+Up) instead of being read every time.
- **Closing a pile view** (Escape) says where focus is again.
- **Gold popups**: "Collector: +50 gold" is no longer read next to "Gained 50 gold, 100 total".
- Quitting a battle to the main menu now fully leaves battle mode.

## Menus

- **Trial switch** on the battle intro: was "Button, off", now "Trial: off. If enabled, additional rule: ... Enable to gain additional reward: ...". F, the game's trial key, now says "Trial on" or "Trial off" too.
- **Settings**: switching tabs says the tab name ("Graphics tab"). Opening Settings says "Settings, Audio tab". The in-game pause menu says "Pause menu".
- **Logbook**: the section tabs are labelled ("Checklist tab", was "Compendium Screen"). On the logbook's front page the arrows now focus the first tab, before they did nothing. Opening the logbook no longer reads "Automatic Railspikes upgraded, Spikedriver Colony upgraded..." for its preview cards. The opening line is shorter and gives the right close key: Q or Backspace. Escape opens the pause menu there.
- **"Main Menu Screen"** is no longer read when you come back to the main menu from Settings or the Logbook. You hear the focused button.
- Empty pile views say "Press Escape to close" instead of "Arrow keys to browse cards".

## Spanish

- All new lines are translated, plus the settings labels that were still in English (Alta calidad, Resolución, Pantalla completa, Temblor de pantalla...).

## Development

- With the debug bridge on (dev mode), the intro logos and video are skipped automatically, and the speech log shows which code spoke each line.
