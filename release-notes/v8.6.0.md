# Monster Train Accessibility v8.6.0

Battle floors on the arrow keys. No more Page Up/Down or summary keys to see what is on each floor.

## Walk the floors with Up and Down

- **Your hand is at the bottom.** In battle, Up climbs from your hand to the bottom floor, the middle floor, the top floor and the pyre room. Down comes back to your hand. At either end the same stop is read again.
- **Moving onto a floor selects it in the game too**, exactly like Page Up/Down, and reads it the same way: capacity, corruption, enchantments, and every unit with its stats.
- **Left and Right on a floor** step through what is on it in battle order: your units back to front, then the enemies front to back. With one unit, Left and Right read it again.
- **The pyre has its own stop** in the pyre room: the Last Shard of the Pyre, with its attack, health and status effects.
- **Enter or F2 on a floor** reads the focused floor or unit with full details.
- **Every unit, enemy and the pyre fills the review buffers.** Ctrl+Up/Down step through status effects with keyword explanations, abilities, enemy intents, and floor corruption or enchantments.
- **Your cards are never touched while a floor is focused.** Left, Right, Enter and E act on the floor, not on the card still focused in your hand. Going back down says "Hand" and the focused card, and Left/Right move through the cards again.
- Number keys still pick a card, F still ends your turn, and each new turn starts on your hand. During combat you can still walk the floors to review them.
- **Shift+K is gone.** The old floor review cursor is now just the arrows.

## Playing cards

- **Up and Down choose the floor** when a card asks for one, like Page Up/Down (which still work).
- Page Up/Down now always read the floor, also when you press them at the top or bottom.

## Spanish

- All new lines are translated, plus "Enchantments" (Encantamientos), which was spoken in English.
